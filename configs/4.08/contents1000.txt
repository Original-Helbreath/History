    *Secure your account!*
Do not share accounts, or your 
information with ANYONE!!! 
A Game Master does NOT need
your account name or password.

    *Secure your items!*
There is no reason for anyone 
to need a screenshot of them 
holding an item you have! 
Don’t share your items!

   *Trading Multiple Items*
If you are going to do a large 
trade involving more than 4 
items, get assistance from 
our GMs. 

      *Need Help?*
If there isn’t a GM online please 
go to http://www.helsupport.com
and register.

TIP: When you find a good drop or 
exchange gear go to a different map 
or log out and back in to save the 
character changes.  This will 
prevent any problems in event of a 
freak server crash.

Note: Keep in mind that you can
