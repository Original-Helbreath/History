Welcome to the Continent(or World) of Helbreath 

In this continent(or World) there are two great

city nations. One is the nation of Aresden where

the God Aresien is worshiped and the nation of

Eldiniel which worships the God Elvine. 

Stand on the marking below and you will be

teleported to the nation of your choice.

For now, you are just a wanderer who can freely

pass through both nations. However, as you chose

one nationality you shall enter into fierce battle

either with Aresden or Elvine who are in war

against one another.

For more detailed instructions of the game, refer

to 'Help Guide'. To view the 'Help Guide' press F1
