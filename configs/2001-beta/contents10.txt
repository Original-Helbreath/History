HELBREATH

INTRODUCTION

We introduce you a new kind of mud game. The scene of
the game belongs to the land's name of the scenery
where the player starts the game in a land called
"Equilibrium". You will participate as one of the
member of the two religion. The Equilibrium land is
divided in two cities, one of them is Elvine who
believes in a unique god called Eldiniel, and the
other one called Aresden who believes in Aresien.
At first you will be a neutral unit, so you will be
able to travel in any one of the two cities. As the
time passes you'll have to depend on one of the
civilization. Your function is to combat against the
other civilization, and also protect the civilian
against the monsters. Your belief and courage, will
let you to combat with the strengh and the witchcraft
of the enemy having a glorious victory in the battle.
