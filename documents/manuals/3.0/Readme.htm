l       HELBREATH Introduction

Introducing ‘HELBREATH’, the sensational new graphic MUG (Multi User Game). HELBREATH refers to the world in which the game takes place. The players take part in the game to end the religious war of Equilibrium. Equilibrium contains two regions – Elvine & Aresden – that serve the Absolute Gods, Eldiniel & Aresien. Which God will you fight for, Eldiniel or Aresien? At first, you are a free traveler, in time however, you must join one region and burden the responsibility of protecting it from opposing forces. To succeed you must hold to your beliefs and trust your intuition and ability.

 

l       HELBREATH Characteristics

 

Helbreath is a 16bit high color game with a smooth screen movement, natural character movement and action, special magic effects, with overflowing sound effects. While other domestic graphic MUDs sacrifice gameplay in order to augment character personality, HELBREATH overcomes these technical limitations allowing it to depict characters in brilliant graphics. In the HELBREATH world as many as 6133 characters can be created with varying physical traits such as hair and skin color. Additionally, the user can render millions of characters by altering optional clothing and weaponry.

 

l       Story Line

 

… Once upon a time in the depths of HELBREATH, there were two Absolute Gods: Eldiniel and Aresien. These two Gods had contact with humankind through the Erisnommire Temple at Mt. Asgarde, located in the western region of HELBREATH. Eldiniel and Aresien ruled the world of 12 primary Gods. When the ‘Descent of Gods’ occurred, the Gods came down and lived in harmony with humankind, making the land fertile and productive. There they released the ‘Divine Power’ among all people . . .

            

In HELBREATH, during the year of 2192 the ‘Prime Event’ took place. The world of HELBREATH fell into deep confusion due to strife between the two Absolute Gods.This event caused a rift between the Gods and humanity. As time passed, the story of the Descent of Gods faded away as legend . . . 

    Equilibrium – the center of HELBREATH – is a peaceful area with the protection and blessing of the Gods. After the detachment of Divine Power, humankind became spiteful of the nature of HELBREATH. Animals became cruel and attacked humans indiscriminately; the abundant landscape became desolate; man’s spirit transformed into something too ruthless to uphold. As the people longed once more for the aid of the Absolute Ones, Equilibrium divided into two sects: those who worshiped Eldiniel and those who worshiped Aresien. As a result, two regions – Elvine and Aresden – emerged, polarizing the landscape . . .

    Without the guidance of the Gods, the absolute evil – Abaddon – once restricted to the eastern region of HELBREATH, started to recover its power. By this process, the forces of evil grew. The deceitful creatures that once lived in the darkness of hell emerged and assailed the villages of HELBREATH. Some say that Abaddon, the land of destruction, is the source of the monsters. Stories arose of missing warriors and magicians who went to Abaddon, not returning. Abaddon became a place feared by the people of HELBREATH…

    As legend of Abaddon is myth, no one knows of the true state of darkness. The one truth is that the forces of evil rise day by day, while humanity busies itself over petty conflict. These chaotic days require real heroes. Heroes who will unite the separated people of HELBREATH and contain the dark powers…
Perhaps you are one of these heroes.

l       Character Specificity

Each character has six specific points, and the specific points can determine the personality of the character. As the level increases, three points of specificity will augment. Specificity points range from 10-200.

STR (Strength)

The STR points define the power of a character. The STR points relate to the maximum load and weight of item. Greater STR points enable faster attack speed and bonus damage.

VIT (Vitality)

The VIT Points define the life force of a character. VIT Points determine the health point of a character. VIT Points are directly proportional to recovery points.

DEX (Dexterity)

The DEX points define the agility of a character. If your DEX points increase, so does attack success and defense rating.

INT (Intelligence)

The INT defines the intelligence of a character. The more INT points you have, the greater your potential for learning magic of a higher circle.

MAG (Magic)

The MAG points define the magical ability of a character. The increase of MAG point results in increase of mana points, extension of recovery points, higher magical attack success, and bonus damage.

CHR (Charisma)

The CHR points define the charisma of a character. Specific CHR point levels enable you to found Guilds, and the increase of CHR points enables you to buy items at cheaper prices.

l       Game Method manufacturing

After you connect in to the game, the player will be guided by this orders.
Moving : Click the left button of the map, and click the place where you want to move.

Running : Press the ‘Shift’ key and click any place and then the character will run.
         In order to run it’s needed the SP (Stamina point).

Stop : After running, click the right button until it stops.
Attack/ Peace mode : Press the Tab key or click the knife icon to convert.
Attack : Click the left button selecting the character where you want to attack. If the mark is out of range you can not attack.
Standstill Attack : To use distance weapons like archery, click the right button. If the distance is too far, it will not be possible to hit.
Differences between enemies and allies : Set the mouse cursor to any character and you will see the difference.
Greeting : If you want to greet someone change to peace mode and click the left button to anyone.
Oppression attack : Enemies or allies, if you want to attack press ctrl key and click the character.
Change direction : Click the right button in to the direction you want
Dash-attack : It means to attack as fast as possible at the same time the enemy. When you are in two blocks of distance
 press Shift+Ctrl and set the mouse cursor to diagonal or in to a straight line direction.

l       Helbreath building structure

 

Helbreath is divided in 3 big maps. In the middle river Abadon is situated the Middleland, at the north Elvine and at the south Aresden. Elvine and Aresden have got many buildings and each one have it’s abilities.

 

Town Portal
Town portal it’s a gate that the player comes from the outside to the village or when the player starts to play.

City Hall
This place has got the responsibility of making the identification for the inhabitants.
Or in case that you catch any criminal gives you the recompense. And also gives you the flag to obtain a territory. When the character has more than 300 ‘Enemy kill’ the official of the hall gives you the hero cloak.
There is an official that works in the city hall. In case that the inhabitant wants to make his identification you can come here. Or in case that you catch any criminal he gives you the recompense. And also the official gives you the flag to obtain a territory.
When the character has more than 300 ‘Enemy kill’ the official gives you the hero cloak.

Guild Hall
To make a new guild or to cancel you can come here. And the guild meeting is made here.

Wizard tower
You can visit here if you want to learn new magic. You need int point and gold.
He teaches magic in a tower inside the city. They say the magician leaves the religion and the city to live by his self-alone, but that’s not correct. The inhabitants there are learning all witchcraft from him, causing a tragic blood war.
Most of people are curious why the magician guild is amounting so much gold.

Shop
You can buy and sell miscellaneous goods as clothes, shoes, etc.
Specially at the beginning when you don’t have money you can pick medicine ingredients such as slim gelly, meets, etc. and sell here. And also you can upgrade your defense power buying clothes.
Shop keeper is a kind woman that sells clothes, shoes, liquid medicine, food, etc. It’s a very important woman to beginner players.

Warehouse
In this place you can entrust your things like weapons and it doesn’t cost any gold.
And it’s important to entrust near the owner because if not the item will drop down.

Blacksmith
You can sell/buy weapons here. And you can repair damaged weapons.
As a retired warrior he is working on fabrication of weapon. Not only weapon but he is also selling books of how to use the weapons. He repairs damaged weapons too.

Bleeding Isle
You should buy the entrance ticket to enter in this area.
There aren’t monsters so many players come here to practice war because the PK rule is not applied. To exit this place you have to use the Recall magic or die.

Entrance to the Middleland
The players can pass through the middleland to get in to another village. There are 2 entrances on each village. Actually, there are lots of flags inside the territory.

Dungeon Level 1
Each village has got 1 entrance to the Dungeon. The Dungeon is restricted until Level 15 because there usually appear two times strength monsters.
You can obtain minerals and bails by digging. In order to increase your mineral gathering you must increase the pick-axe skill, then just attack the mark.
In addition, you must be careful with different color monsters because they are a little bit strong then the normal one.

Barrack
You can train by attacking the Dummy. After level 40 you can’t enter in this place.

Dungeon Level 2
It’s a big place situated in the Middleland, and the entrance is allowed for players of level 40 or more. And you also can obtain minerals here.
Appears a lot of monsters, and in some dungeons there is an entrance gate to the dungeon level 3.

Dungeon Level 3
The dungeon level 3 made with the structure of material and the mirror remains the ancient times. Here appear a magic monster called Liche. It’s not possible to obtain items but killing the Liche you can obtain rare items.

Resurrection Zone
Each village has got it’s resurrection Zone. When you die re-start here.
